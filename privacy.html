Spellbinder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Spellbinder is a personal hobby web app for tracking Magic: The Gathering card collections and trading with other members. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app stores and what it does with it.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. Inside the app we store the collection data you create: your binders, the cards in them, your display name, profile color, and any profile picture you upload.

How it is used

Your information is used only to run the app: to show you your collection, and — only for binders and profiles you choose to make public — to show them to other signed-in members. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Visibility you control

Accounts and binders are private by default. Nothing about your collection is visible to other members unless you turn on a public setting yourself. You can switch anything back to private at any time.

Where it is stored

App data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage). Card data and prices come from public sources such as Scryfall. Google sign-in is handled by Google; we never see or store your Google password.

Deleting your data

You can delete your binders and cards inside the app at any time. To have your account and all associated data removed, contact the app owner at the email below and we will delete it.
